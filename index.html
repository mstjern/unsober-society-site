Unsober Society

Check in with your crew.

Unsober Society is an iPhone app for private groups of friends on a night out. Snap a photo, say where you are, and pick which of your groups sees it. Your friends get a push and answer with a 🍻 or a 🖕, and the map shows where your groups checked in over the last 12 hours. Only members of the groups you choose ever see a check-in, and people who join a group later never see what came before.

The app is only for people of legal drinking age where they live. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Please drink responsibly, and never drink and drive.
