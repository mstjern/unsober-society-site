Support

Questions, problems, or something you want removed? Write to magnus.stjernstrom@gmail.com.

Report a check-in or a photo

Tap the … button on the check-in, in the feed or on the map, then Report check-in, or Report <name>’s photo to report their profile photo. Choose a reason: offensive, nudity, harassment, spam, or other (with an optional note). It is hidden from you straight away. If two different people report it, it is hidden from everyone until we have reviewed it. We review every report, normally within 24 hours.

Report or block a person

Tap the … button on one of their check-ins, or open a group you share and find them under Members, then choose Report or Block.

If you own a group, a request to join it has the same … button, so you can report or block someone before you approve them.

When you block someone, you stop seeing each other’s check-ins, reactions, profile photos and places on the map, and neither of you gets notifications about the other. They are not told. Blocking does not remove anyone from a group you share.

Report a group

Open the group from your photo at the top right of the feed, tap the … button, then Report group, for example for an offensive group name.

Unblock someone

Tap your profile photo (or initial) at the top of the feed, open Blocked people, and unblock them there.

Delete a check-in

Tap the … button on your own check-in, then Delete check-in. Its photo is deleted too.

Leave a group

Tap your profile photo (or initial) at the top of the feed, open the group, then tap Leave group. If you own it, ownership passes to the member who has been in it longest. If you own it and are its last member, the group is deleted. You stop seeing its check-ins straight away.

Delete your account

Tap your profile photo (or initial) at the top of the feed, then Delete account. The app explains what is removed, and Apple asks you to confirm by signing in once more; if you cancel, nothing is deleted. The Privacy Policy lists what is removed.

Notifications

The app asks once, after you first sign in. To change your answer later, open iOS Settings, then Unsober, then Notifications.

Location

Location is optional and is used only while the app is open. Without it you type the venue yourself and your check-in has no map point. You can also turn off Show on map for any check-in. To change the permission, open iOS Settings, then Unsober, then Location. The Privacy Policy explains what is shared.

Contact

For questions, reports about content, or requests about your data, write to magnus.stjernstrom@gmail.com.
