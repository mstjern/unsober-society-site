No objectionable content, no abusive users

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Do not post, or use your name or profile photo for:

The same rules apply to reactions, group names and display names.

Reports, blocking and removal

  • You can report any check-in, profile photo or person from the app. Something you report is hidden from you straight away. Something reported by two different people is hidden from everyone until we have reviewed it.
  • We review every report, normally within 24 hours.
  • We remove objectionable content, and we suspend or remove the accounts of people who post it or abuse others, without warning. A suspended account cannot post, react, join groups or change its profile photo.
  • You can block anyone. You then stop seeing each other’s check-ins, reactions and profile photos, and neither of you gets notifications about the other. The person you block is not told.
  • We do not tell anyone who reported them.

You can delete your account at any time in the app: tap your profile photo (or initial) at the top of the feed, then tap Delete account. The Privacy Policy says what is removed.
